# Optional owner-supplied oranges photo

No photo is currently supplied or licensed for bundling. The app defaults to the
original `oranges.svg` illustration. No prototype photo, stock image, downloaded
image, or remote fallback is included.

To enable the optional **Your photo** source, place your own photo of loose oranges
at `src/assets/oranges-photo.jpg` and update this record before building or sharing:

- Creator / copyright owner:
- Original source and capture date:
- License or explicit permission to bundle and publish:
- Attribution required:
- Resizing / optimization performed:
- Original and bundled dimensions:

Use a reasonably sized, optimized JPEG with no baked-in netting. Keep its natural
aspect ratio; the app does not crop it. Restart development or rebuild after
adding/removing the file. **Illustration** remains the default and Reset source.
Adding this file does not grant a license or verify ownership automatically.

This JPEG path is deliberately not ignored by Git: it is reserved for your own
photo, which can be committed and deployed with its origin/permission record.
Vite bundles the photo when present. Remove unknown-license diagnostic images
and rebuild before sharing.
The temporary prototype-photo A/B on 20 October 2026 grants no distribution rights;
its copied JPEG was removed after the comparison. No photo is shipped.
